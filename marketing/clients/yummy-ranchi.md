2. Review text points to potential message themes around pizza, value, taste, and hospitality. Ask the owner which items and service strengths are most representative before using them in promotional copy.
3. The small review count means testimonials should be quoted only with platform-compliant attribution and permission where needed. Do not solicit only positive reviews, offer rewards for positive reviews, or create reviews.
