# Café & Restaurant Local Marketing

Use this skill with the workspace chief-of-staff instructions where available. Optimize for accurate information, a clear customer journey, operationally realistic promises, and repeatable learning—not vanity metrics or needless complexity.

## Intake and evidence

1. Identify business, location, target audience, desired business outcome, timeframe, budget, assets, and approval owner. Reuse known client files.
2. Gather relevant public information and cite the source URL and review date. Separate directly observed facts from customer opinions, owner-confirmed facts, and hypotheses.
3. Treat menus, prices, opening hours, availability, accessibility, dietary/allergen statements, service mode, delivery, and booking details as changeable or safety-sensitive. Confirm with the owner before using them in public claims.
4. Do not infer service quality, customer demographics, or a business's status from a small sample. State when a view or review sample is limited.

## Select the work

For a broad promotion audit, examine five connected areas:

- Local discovery: accurate name/category/location/phone/hours, useful photos, menu, and working action links.
- Reviews and trust: recurring themes, neutral invitations to all customers, and considerate replies.
- Website and conversion: mobile clarity, menu/location/hours, a primary order/book/call/visit action, readability, and useful local information.
- Brand, content, and offer: audience and occasion, authentic strengths, consistent visual/tone choices, clear CTA, and usage rights for assets.
- In-person experience: arrival, ordering, waiting, service, ambience, product consistency, issue recovery, and a credible reason to return.

Prioritize no more than five opportunities. For each, state evidence, action, customer/business benefit, effort, approval needed, and a realistic success signal. Sort into Now, Next, Later.

## Ethical reviews and claims

Never create, buy, suppress, or selectively solicit positive reviews. Never gate review links or offer an incentive conditional on a positive review. Suggest a neutral request to all customers through allowed channels. Draft public replies without disclosing private details, arguing, or making unapproved promises. Quote reviews sparingly with clear attribution and permission when required.

Avoid fabricated testimonials, fake scarcity, unsupported superlatives, health claims, provenance claims, awards, or performance guarantees. Do not claim that a tactic will increase revenue without evidence. Make promotions deliverable by staff, stock, hours, price, and capacity.

## Produce useful deliverables

For an audit, use the workspace `templates/cafe-audit.md` where available. For a campaign, include goal, audience/visit occasion, confirmed offer or message, channel, ready-to-review copy, CTA/destination, schedule, needed assets, operational check, approval owner, and a simple success measure. Clearly label drafts and record publication/result details only after approval.

Recommend specialist role briefs from `agents/` only when they add value. The chief of staff should coordinate parallel work only for independent assignments and return one ranked, owner-ready plan.

## External actions and privacy

Prepare drafts freely. Get explicit owner approval before publishing, spending, editing a live listing/site, replying on behalf of the business, contacting customers or creators, or making an irreversible change. Do not store passwords, payment details, or unnecessary personal/customer data in project files.
